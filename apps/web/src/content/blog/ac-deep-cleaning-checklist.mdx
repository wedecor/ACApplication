A "₹299 AC service" usually means a 20-minute filter-rinse and a quick
visual check. That's fine as routine maintenance — but it isn't a
**deep-clean**, and confusing the two is why people complain about
weak cooling after "just getting the AC serviced".

Here's what a proper chemical deep-clean covers:

## 1. Dismount or seal-cover the indoor unit

The technician should either fully dismount the indoor unit (best for
splits) or use a coil-cover (a plastic apron that channels dirty water
into a bucket). If neither happens — water and old residue will drip
onto your wall and floor.

## 2. Foam coil cleaner on the evaporator

A pH-neutral or mildly alkaline foam cleaner is sprayed across the
indoor coil. It expands, lifts dust and bio-film, and is rinsed off
with a low-pressure pump. **Reject** any technician who:

- Uses a caustic / industrial degreaser on aluminium fins (corrosion).
- Sprays water directly on the PCB / control board without covering it.

## 3. Blower wheel cleaning

The blower wheel (the long cylindrical fan under the coil) accumulates
the heaviest bio-film. It must be hand-cleaned with a brush or rotated
under spray. Skipping this step is the #1 reason cooling stays weak
after a "service".

## 4. Drainpipe flush + deodoriser

A small amount of biocide is poured into the drain pan, then the pipe
is flushed under pressure. This kills the algae and prevents the
mid-summer "AC smells musty" complaint.

## 5. Outdoor unit pressure-wash

The condenser fins (outdoor unit) get pressure-washed at low PSI —
high pressure bends fins and tanks efficiency permanently. Bent fins
should be combed back with a fin-comb.

## 6. Anti-bacterial fogging

A UV / biocide aerosol fogged through the coil and blower kills the
bio-film spores you can't see. This is what removes the "wet-cloth"
smell from older units.

## 7. Performance benchmark

A real deep-clean ends with a temperature-differential reading: room
temperature minus outlet temperature. A healthy split unit should
show **10-13 °C** ΔT after deep-cleaning. Anything below 8 °C points
at low gas, dirty filters or under-sized capacity for the room.

The technician should share these readings on WhatsApp before leaving.
